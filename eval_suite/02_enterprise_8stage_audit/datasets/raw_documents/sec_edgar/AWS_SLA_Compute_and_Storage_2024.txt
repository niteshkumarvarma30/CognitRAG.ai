AMAZON WEB SERVICES — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
COVERED SERVICES: AMAZON EC2, AMAZON EBS (AMAZON COMPUTE SLA)
OFFICIAL URL: https://aws.amazon.com/compute/sla/
EFFECTIVE: CALENDAR YEAR 2024 / 2025

Service Commitment:
AWS will use commercially reasonable efforts to make Amazon EC2 and Amazon EBS available with a 
Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99% during any monthly billing cycle across Multiple Availability 
Zones within the same region (the Region-Level SLA).

Region-Level Service Credits:
Monthly Uptime Percentage                                  Service Credit Percentage
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Less than 99.99% but equal to or greater than 99.0%        10% Service Credit
Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0%         30% Service Credit
Less than 95.0%                                           100% Service Credit
====================================================================================

Instance-Level Service Credits (Single EC2 Instance):
Instance-Level Uptime Percentage                           Service Credit Percentage
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Less than 99.5% but equal to or greater than 99.0%         10% Service Credit
Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0%         30% Service Credit
Less than 95.0%                                           100% Service Credit
====================================================================================

Credit Request Procedures:
Service Credits are calculated as a percentage of the monthly bill for Amazon EC2 in the affected AWS region.
Claims must be submitted by opening a case in the AWS Support Center by the end of the second billing cycle.
