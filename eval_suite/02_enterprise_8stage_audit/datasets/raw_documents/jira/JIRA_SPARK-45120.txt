Title: TaskSchedulerImpl deadlocks on stage cancellation during executor loss

Description:
Under heavy spot-instance termination events in the Kubernetes cluster (cluster-id: k8s-prod-us-east-1), TaskSchedulerImpl deadlocks when an executor lost notification arrives concurrently with a DAGScheduler stage cancellation request.

Thread dump analysis reveals a lock inversion between:
1) Thread-1 (DAGScheduler event loop) holding `DAGScheduler.lock` and acquiring `TaskSchedulerImpl.synchronized`.
2) Thread-2 (TaskSchedulerImpl status update thread) holding `TaskSchedulerImpl.synchronized` and waiting for `DAGScheduler.stageIdToActiveJobIds`.

Impact:
Entire PySpark ETL pipelines hang indefinitely at 99% stage completion, causing cascading SLA breaches for morning financial batch reconciliations.

Resolution:
Resolved in GitHub PR #4512. Refactored `TaskSchedulerImpl.scala` to decouple stage abort handling from the inner synchronized task state map, introducing a lock-free `ConcurrentHashMap` for active job tracking. Verified zero deadlocks across 500 simulated worker terminations.
