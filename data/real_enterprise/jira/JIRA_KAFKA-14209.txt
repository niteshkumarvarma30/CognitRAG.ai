Title: Consumer group coordinator rebalance storm during broker rolling restart

Description:
During standard bi-weekly rolling AMI updates of Kafka brokers in cluster `kafka-prod-core`, consumer groups with >1,000 members experience persistent rebalance storms. The group coordinator fails to complete `JoinGroup` and `SyncGroup` phases before `max.poll.interval.ms` (300,000ms) expires.

Root Cause:
When the broker hosting the `__consumer_offsets` partition leader restarts, the new coordinator election forces all consumers to rejoin simultaneously. The single-threaded group coordinator state machine suffers lock contention on offset commit verification.

Resolution:
Resolved in GitHub PR #1421. Added randomized jitter (exponential backoff between 500ms and 5000ms) to consumer `join.group.backoff.ms` and parallelized coordinator heartbeat verification in `GroupCoordinator.scala`. Eliminates rebalance thrashing completely.
