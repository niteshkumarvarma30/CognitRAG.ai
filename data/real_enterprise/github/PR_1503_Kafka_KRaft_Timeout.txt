Related Issues: Resolves KAFKA-15022

Overview:
Increases `controller.quorum.fetch.timeout.ms` from 2,000ms to 6,000ms and optimizes Raft snapshot serialization for clusters managing >50k partitions.

Configuration:
```properties
+ controller.quorum.fetch.timeout.ms = 6000
+ controller.quorum.election.timeout.ms = 10000
+ metadata.log.segment.bytes = 67108864
```

Verification:
Simulated 75,000 partition churn in staging. Controller leader maintained continuous quorum without dropped heartbeats.
