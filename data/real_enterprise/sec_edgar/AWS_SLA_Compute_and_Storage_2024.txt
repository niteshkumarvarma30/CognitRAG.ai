AMAZON WEB SERVICES — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
COVERED SERVICES: AMAZON EC2, AMAZON EBS, AND AMAZON S3
EFFECTIVE: CALENDAR YEAR 2024

Service Commitment:
AWS will use commercially reasonable efforts to make Amazon EC2 and Amazon EBS available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99% during any monthly billing cycle across Multiple Availability Zones within the same region.

Service Credits:
Monthly Uptime Percentage:
- Less than 99.99% but equal to or greater than 99.0%: 10% Service Credit
- Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0%: 25% Service Credit
- Less than 95.0%: 100% Service Credit

Exclusions:
The Service Commitment does not apply to any unavailability, suspension, or termination of Covered Services:
1. Caused by factors outside our reasonable control, including any force majeure event or Internet access problems beyond the demarcation point of AWS.
2. Resulting from any actions or inactions of customer or third parties.
3. Resulting from customer's software, configurations, or hardware.
