---

# 2. What CognitRAG Is

CognitRAG connects fragmented enterprise information and transforms it into structured, retrievable, relationship-aware, permission-controlled knowledge.

The existing architecture combines:

- Supabase/PostgreSQL + pgvector
- Neo4j
- hybrid BM25 + vector retrieval
- reranking
- LangGraph orchestration
- semantic definitions
- RBAC/RLS
- source metadata
- streaming responses

The customer should experience this as one product:

> **A Company Brain that understands how the organization works.**

---

# 3. What CognitRAG Is NOT

Avoid positioning CognitRAG primarily as:

- another chatbot
- another enterprise search box
- another company wiki
- another document management system
- a generic RAG wrapper
- a generic AI assistant
- an LLM-powered Notion replacement

These descriptions focus on individual features rather than the larger system.

# 23. The "Why Not Search?" Answer

If someone asks:

> "Why can't I just search Slack and Notion?"

Answer:

> Search helps you find where information might exist. CognitRAG is designed to connect the information across systems, understand the relationships between entities and decisions, and return an answer with evidence and permission-aware context.

---

# 24. The "Why Not RAG?" Answer

> Standard RAG is excellent for retrieving relevant text. CognitRAG extends the retrieval workflow with structured entities, relationships, company-defined semantics, permissions, reranking, and graph-aware context so the system can reason over organizational structure rather than only similar passages.

Do not claim "zero hallucinations" as an absolute marketing promise.

Prefer:

> **Designed for verifiable, grounded enterprise answers.**

# 43. Enterprise Objection Handling

## "We already use Notion."

Response:

> Notion stores knowledge. CognitRAG connects knowledge across the systems where your organization actually operates and can continuously surface relationships and changes.

---

## "We already use Slack search."

Response:

> Slack search finds messages. CognitRAG is designed to connect information from Slack with systems such as Jira, GitHub, documents, and structured data.

---

## "We already have RAG."

Response:

> CognitRAG can serve as the semantic context layer around retrieval, adding graph relationships, controlled definitions, permissions, and evidence.

---

## "Is our data secure?"

Response:

> CognitRAG is designed around permission-aware retrieval and database-level access controls. The exact security posture depends on the deployed configuration and implemented controls.
