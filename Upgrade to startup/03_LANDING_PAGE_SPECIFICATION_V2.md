# 03 V2 — CognitRAG Premium Landing Page Specification
## Startup Launch Website — Framer-Style Visual Storytelling

> **Status:** Official landing-page specification.
>
> **Visual source of truth:** `04_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`
>
> **Core direction:** Premium, spacious, editorial, product-led AI startup website with CognitRAG's own Company Brain identity.

---

# 1. Landing Page Mission

The CognitRAG landing page has one job:

> Make a visitor understand the problem, see the product, trust the approach, and take the next step.

The visitor should move through:

```text
I recognize this problem.
        ↓
I understand CognitRAG.
        ↓
I can see the product.
        ↓
I understand why it is different.
        ↓
I trust it.
        ↓
I want to try it / talk to the team.
```

The website must sell the **outcome and product experience**, not the backend architecture.

---

# 2. Primary Conversion

For the startup launch:

> **Get Early Access**

or, if the sales process is already active:

> **Book a Demo**

Recommended primary CTA:

```text
[ Get Early Access ]
```

Recommended secondary CTA:

```text
[ See How It Works → ]
```

The exact CTA can change later after customer acquisition is validated.

---

# 3. The Five-Second Test

Within approximately five seconds, the visitor should understand:

```text
WHAT
Company Brain

PROBLEM
Company knowledge is fragmented.

VALUE
Turn company knowledge into trusted context
for people and AI agents.

ACTION
Get Early Access
```

If the visitor thinks CognitRAG is simply another chatbot, the landing page has failed.
