# 20 — CognitRAG Startup GTM & Customer Acquisition Strategy
## From Company Brain Product → First Customers → Repeatable Revenue

> **Status:** Master startup go-to-market specification.
>
> **Product foundation:** `01`–`19`
>
> **Core positioning:** CognitRAG — The Company Brain
>
> **Purpose:** Define how CognitRAG should find, convince, onboard, convert, retain, and expand its first customers.
>
> **Important:** This document is a startup operating strategy. Customer segments, pricing, channels, and claims should be validated through real market conversations rather than treated as proven facts.

---

# 1. GTM Objective

The first objective is not:

> Get thousands of signups.

The first objective is:

> **Find a small number of companies with a painful knowledge/context problem, solve one workflow extremely well, prove measurable value, and turn those successes into repeatable sales.**

The initial motion should therefore be:

```text
Customer Discovery
      ↓
Design Partner
      ↓
Pilot
      ↓
Paid Pilot
      ↓
Expansion
      ↓
Repeatable Sales
```

# 23. CognitRAG vs Knowledge Base

Knowledge bases are often:

```text

```

```text

```

and can organize important context into reusable knowledge.

The product does not have to replace the customer's existing knowledge base.
